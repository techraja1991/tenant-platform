Auto-send time

Each due date is scoped to exactly one billing month. A tenant can reschedule — setting a new date for the same month keeps the old one in history (marked superseded) and the newest becomes active. Automated messages go out daily at the auto-send time: on the day a date is set, one day before it, and on the due date (short reminder + the full template hisab). Sends are skipped when the month's outstanding is already Rs. 0.
